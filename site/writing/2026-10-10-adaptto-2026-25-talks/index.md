## Why I went through all 25 talks

adaptTo() is Europe's developer conference for Adobe Experience Manager (AEM), organized by diva-e Conclusion. I was just there for the 2026 edition, its 15th anniversary. It was one of the best conferences I've ever attended. Engineers and researchers from Adobe were there, along with engineers and researchers from all over the world. Katie Phillips from Best Buy and I gave a talk there too.

So I gave each of the 25 full talks about 30 seconds on camera. I left out the lightning talks. For each talk I cover three things:

- What the talk is
- Why you should watch it
- My take

It's meant to help you pick which recordings to watch once they're up. Each take is my own limited understanding from the room. Some of it may be wrong, and a few takes are a little spicy.

Below, every talk has its slot, its speakers, links to its page, slides and moment in the video, and the slide I had on screen while I gave my take.
