AI Agent-Driven Content Migration for Edge Delivery Services

adaptTo() 2026 · Berlin · with Katie Phillips

September 29, 2026 · Conference session

AI-assisted migration into DA.live and EDS, with orchestration, evaluation, and human review.

Watch the recorded talk

Slide: Jack Jin and Katie Phillips, adaptTo() 2026.

What it is

Our talk, Katie Phillips from Best Buy and me, the first talk of the second day: AI agents that migrate content to Edge Delivery Services.

From our slides: one framework for any content process, with any trigger, any route through the agents, and four places a human can step in.

My take

Three things I'd call out:

  • Like most talks at the conference, ours is very agentic. It's also AI using AI, where most of the other talks were about humans using AI.
  • We think about evals.
  • It's a multi-step process, and the AI agents share a common working process.

That fits content migration well, because you want to run things in parallel. Sometimes you spin up tens or hundreds of agents to migrate a bunch of pages, run evals, throw the results away and do it again. AI using AI can do that in a very scalable way.
